Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please find enclosed the manuscript "Day-Ahead Load Forecasting Under Rapid
Behind-the-Meter Solar Growth: An Open Benchmark Against a System Operator's
Official Forecast in Brazil", which I submit for consideration for publication
in IEEE Access.

The rapid growth of behind-the-meter photovoltaic generation is changing the
load seen by transmission system operators worldwide, yet open forecasting
models are rarely compared with the forecasts actually used in operation. This
manuscript provides such a comparison for the four subsystems of the Brazilian
interconnected system, where behind-the-meter generation grew from about 5% to
one third of the midday load in five years.

The main contributions are:

1. An open, fully reproducible day-ahead load forecasting benchmark built
   exclusively from public operator data (2012-2026), with an operationally
   realistic information set, a test period of almost three years never used
   for model design, and Diebold-Mariano significance testing.
2. A head-to-head comparison with the operator's official day-ahead forecast:
   a weather-free ensemble of ARX, gradient boosting and neural network models
   achieved a MAPE of 2.44% versus 2.90% for the official forecast, with
   significant improvements in three of the four subsystems.
3. Evidence of a mechanism of general relevance: the official forecast showed
   a systematic midday under-forecast that deepened as behind-the-meter
   generation grew, whereas the data-driven models remained nearly unbiased.
4. A quantification of the effect of calibration windows and refitting
   frequencies under drift, and a comparison of quantile gradient boosting
   with adaptive conformal prediction intervals under delayed feedback.

The work falls within the scope of IEEE Access in power and energy systems and
machine learning. All data are public, and the code that reproduces every
table and figure is available at https://github.com/izialber/artigo-ieee.
